An AI companion that plays Terraria with you. It follows you, fights beside you, does what you are doing, takes orders, and grows with your progression through its own upgrade tree.

Singleplayer only. While a companion is up, enemies spawn twice as often: two of you clear a screen twice as fast, so the world sends twice as much.

Early development. Right now: type /companion in chat to spawn a companion that follows you around the world.
