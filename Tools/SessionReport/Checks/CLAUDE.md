# Checks — finding questions the record can answer

Each question the record can answer is one class implementing `ICheck`. `Program.cs` registers them; a missing schema column skips the check as reduced coverage via `ICheckCoverage`.

**`TheReachFloodSettles` asks whether the reach flood ever finishes, and it exists because nothing else in the report would say so.** Every optional activity asks the flood rather than searching for itself, and an unsettled flood is the one answer that correctly stops work from starting — so a flood that stays unsettled stops all of it while every offer on every row says the honest thing. Nothing shouts, which is why a check has to ask. It is graded Potential rather than Definitive: an unsettled flood is a state the design has a correct answer for, not a contradiction. It allows a gap of thirty rows inside a stretch, because the flood flickers by design — it finishes, a dig raises the terrain revision, it starts again — and a run broken by those single settled rows is several stretches that each fall under the threshold, which is how a stall hides from its own check. On the 2026-09-14 capture it reports 6,478 rows in one stretch from tick 1.

**`ColumnsHoldWhatTheyClaim` unions its own set of known-textual columns with whatever a capture declares, rather than letting the declaration replace it.** The writer's `# text_columns=` line is a hand-maintained string beside the header builder, so every new textual column is two places to remember and the second is the one that gets forgotten; `torch_reason` was forgotten in schema 0.31.0, and because the declaration replaced this set outright, every capture carrying it reported all of its rows as cells that failed to parse as numbers. A curated set can only ever mark a column textual, never numeric, so the union cannot hide a real fault, and it reads an already-written capture correctly instead of only the next one.

```
Checks/
├─ CheckCourseDecisions.cs            a course decision accounts for its own search: tallies within the refusals they summarise, a purpose only off a bound step, every decision naming a reason
├─ CheckDecisionContracts.cs          arrival must satisfy following, and must lie inside its purpose's success region
├─ CheckDecisionStability.cs          a chosen behaviour lasts long enough to accomplish something
├─ CheckFailedMethods.cs              a method tried more than once with no credited effect
├─ CheckFollowProgress.cs             follow objective open but body is stalled or receding
├─ CheckOffersAttemptsAndGrants.cs    selections, attempts and grants read by process-wide identity, and the course-era replacement for the chooser's retained-selection rule
├─ CheckTheBody.cs                    a body with a route ahead of it moves, and being unable to reach him is noticed
├─ CheckTheCensusAgainstItsBinder.cs  a domain the census admitted usable survived the binder in the same decision
├─ CheckTheChoices.cs                 choice rows reflect what the activity owner recorded
├─ CheckTheDecisionAudit.cs           whether the decision audit was wired to anything the session that wrote this capture actually decided, read from the capture's closing counts rather than from a row
├─ CheckTheFight.cs                   hands work while threatened; a hitting fight keeps its score; a fight held over targets its binder cannot see; a crowd is priced from here
├─ CheckTheFrame.cs                   the whole update against the engine's own timestep, reported with its split rather than as a share alone; the brain cost is read from the previous *update* rather than the previous line of the file, the guard `Measures/CLAUDE.md`'s `MeasureTheFrame.cs` section states
├─ CheckTheInstrument.cs              the body is never held in place by something the record does not name
├─ CheckThePlayersReference.cs        his own cursor's dark tile left unlit, and a usable hunt beside him idle, while keeping company won
├─ CheckTheRecord.cs                  identities parse correctly, columns hold what they claim, the capture closed
├─ CheckTheWeaponKnowledge.cs         every companion shot paired with the flight its projectile flew, per projectile type, with the law named
└─ CheckTravelEpisodes.cs             journeys take the time they were proven; stops have reasons
```

Each check earns a finding class at `Definitive` (a contract contradiction the recorder and the check cannot both be right about), `Potential` (evidence reduced by capture damage or missing schema), or no finding when the schema is too old. An Oddity signals that the recording never exercised that finding kind; it is not a defect. The capture's arrival radius, stand-site tile spans and torch-illumination hysteresis are measured against the checks that verify them, making them part of the recorder's own contract. `TravelEvidence.First` is the one place the gate lives for new schema features.

`CheckTravelEpisodes.cs` asks about a whole journey rather than about one segment, and it is now the only movement-timing check there is: a route's segments can each be flown at pace while the journey costs four times its price, because the time goes into the gaps between them. A stop's reasons are the producer's own three, in its precedence — `during-replan` for a route replaced under a still body, `against-wall` for a body pressed against a wall throughout, and `other` for anything it cannot explain, which is the one worth reading. `JourneysTakeTheTimeTheyWereProven` reports per request kind what the journeys took against their proven total and against the player's own ticks over the same ground, and `TheBodyStopsOnItsOwnRoute` reports the stops, their rate per minute of travel and their split by reason. The player figure is the only reference in this tool the brain did not produce; a `-` there is missing trail coverage, never a player who was slower. A journey's duration excludes the ticks the body spent downed and the finding says how many were excluded, because a death inside a journey is not the journey being slow and a downing outlasts most journeys.

Both are Oddity throughout on purpose. No capture yet carries one of these occurrences, so any threshold would be grown from an imagined failure and would fire on the shape its author imagined — which the folder's own rule names as the thing to avoid. They state the ratios; the first real capture showing a journey costing several times its price is what earns a graded check, and each finding says what such a check would have to separate before it could grade anything. `TravelEvidence.First` is the one place the gate lives, and the fixtures derive their capture stamps from it — the running captures from the gate itself and the skipping one from a minor below — because a fixture holding the literal schema reads as older than the gate the moment the mod's schema passes it, and the captures meant to run would then skip while the assertions on their findings failed for a reason unconnected to what they test. Neither new kind appears in `DescribeGodsEyeEvents`'s fixed list of meaningful kinds, so a journey and a stop are absent from the per-episode narrative and reachable only through these two checks and the HTML's own by-kind grouping, which builds its groups from whatever kinds the file holds.

**`AHittingFightKeptItsScore` and `CombatWasPricedInACrowd` grade the two 0.30.5 play roots: combat raw held and final near zero while another job ran (the reunion dump), and `plan_reason=budget-cut` while he was in danger with several hostiles (the crowd that deleted combat). Both are Potential. They skip by missing column rather than reading clean on an older capture.

The player is the one reference the brain did not produce, and two checks read him directly.** `TorchesGoWhereHisCursorWould` finds stretches where keeping company won while the tile the player's own smart cursor would offer for a torch read dark and stayed the same tile throughout — the same tile is what "it stayed unlit" means in a record that cannot see the torch go in — and reports the stage lighting refused that tile at, because a sealed stand is a correct refusal and a lit, placer-refused or search-cut stage is a rule or a search disagreeing with his cursor. `HuntsWorthTakingAreTaken` finds stretches where keeping company won while hunting rated its offer usable, the nearest reachable hostile was near, and the player stood idle, and reports hunting's raw, final and time factors with its funnel's furthest stage. Both are Potential, and every threshold is a named constant in the check carrying the reason it has that size. They meet an older capture differently. The cursor check needs columns only schema 0.35.0 writes, so it skips a 0.34.0 capture by name. The hunt check needs only columns 0.34.0 already records, and reads the time factor and the funnel where the capture has them, so it runs on a 0.34.0 capture and leaves those two readings out. Read against the 14:16 capture of 15 September, its four conditions held together on 234 rows but never for more than 26 consecutive ticks, so it reported nothing. That is a fact about that afternoon's record, not a fault in the check.

**A check's own name lands in the coverage block of every report, including the multi-run one, so it shares a namespace with that report's vocabulary.** `MultiRunReport` says "refused cross-run joins" when two captures came from different code, and its self-test asserts that word is absent from a clean join by plain substring — so naming a check "did a refused step…" once failed a multi-run assertion about provenance, in a report about something else, with nothing in either file wrong. That check is gone now and the collision with it, but the rule it bought is not: a new check's name is worth reading against the words the reports already use before it is settled.

**`TheDecisionAuditRanOnTheDecisionsTheCaptureHolds` reads whether the decision audit ran at all, never what it said.** The audit hangs off `RecordCourseTrace.Record` and takes its inputs through a delegate `ReadLiveCourseForAudit.Install` hands it from the recorder's `Load`; remove the hook and every decision is recorded with nothing audited, skip the install and every decision is audited against no inputs, and both failures look exactly like a session with nothing to report, which is this folder's own standing trap — a diagnostic reading a producer that moved. Its evidence is the capture's closing trailer rather than a row, because the audit's own counts are counts of the session and first exist at schema 0.45.0; its declared column, `frame_ms`, is a witness it never reads, there only so an older capture skips by name instead of reading an absence as a zero.

## The finding six people made by hand and no check asked for

`ACensusAdmissionSurvivesItsBinder` is the check this folder was missing on 22 September 2026, and its absence is worth a paragraph because nothing in the report was broken. Both halves of the contradiction were already in the record, in one field of one occurrence — the `decision` detail carries `course-admitted:combat=usable:3` beside `course-refused:target-capture-missing=12` — and a person had to notice that those cannot both be true of one frozen observation. Six independent readings of that capture each noticed it, each by a script written for the occasion, and the reader printed neither half.

It is a contradiction rather than a low score because the two refusal strings are one test in two files: `CombatCourseOpportunity` returns Unresolved `target-capture-missing` when a target fact's evidence is not `Observed`, and `AssistanceOpportunityBinder` refuses `assistance-target-unresolved` on the same condition, and the census that admitted the domain read the same frozen observation. So a domain admitted usable whose every order is refused for want of an observed target is the discovery side and the binding side of one decision disagreeing about one fact.

**The join is two records and the grade follows which of them a decision carries.** The refusal tallies ride on the typed `course-decision` payload, written every tick; the admissions ride on the `decision` occurrence, which is periodic — 469 of them against 2,340 decisions on that capture. A decision carrying both is the observed contradiction and is Definitive; a decision carrying only the refusals is read against the last admission before it, which is how the producer's own `Admitted` list behaves while a retained course is carried and is an inference all the same, so those are counted apart inside the finding's own text. **An admission that moved between two `decision` occurrences is invisible to the join**, and the finding says so rather than presenting the carried count as observation. On the 22 September capture it reports 555 combat decisions over 842 records refusing 6,299 orders from tick 829 (216 records observed) and 554 collect decisions over 833 records refusing 9,337 from tick 842 (215 observed).

Three things about that paragraph were each wrong once, and each was invisible until somebody wrote a script against the same capture.

**The carry is from the last admission at or before the decision, not from one landing on its own tick.** The two producers keep different cadences, so 230 of that capture's 468 admission ticks carry no `course-decision` payload at all; a per-tick lookup dropped every one of them and left the decisions afterwards reading an older census. It changed none of that capture's numbers, because the admissions it dropped repeat what the previous one said, and that is exactly why nothing would have caught it. The fixture carries an admission on a tick no payload shares.

**A record is not a decision, and the finding names both.** A retained course traces a payload per tick, so its 842 combat records are 555 decisions under the same `choice_id` identity the timeline keys on, and the 6,299 refusals are that decision's tally re-recorded — an upper bound on distinct orders rather than a count. All six hand readings of the capture said "842 decisions" and this check inherited it. Where the capture predates `choice_id` being the course's, at 0.43.0, the finding counts records and says that is what they are.

**The observed-versus-inferred grade is the check's centrepiece and was the one rule with no red behind it.** A planted `bool observed = true` left the suite green, because every fixture arm held a decision carrying both halves; a carried-only contradiction graded Definitive would drive the process exit code off an inference. There is an arm for it now.

Three further properties are the reusable part. The reason names the **binder** and not the site, so `assistance-target-unresolved` behind two usable assistance domains cannot be attributed to either and the finding counts those decisions and says so. From schema 0.45.0 the recorder asks the same question from the producer's side as a `contract-violation` of kind `census-admitted-binder-refused`, keyed on the observation ordinal, and the finding states agreement; **a domain the tripwire fired on that this reader found nothing in is its own finding**, because two instruments disagreeing is a fact about the instruments rather than about the play. And the refusal-to-domain table here mirrors `AuditDecisionContracts.RefusalFor` in the other direction, with the self-test reading all five literals out of the three producing files by path, because a table that drifts from its producer reports a clean run for ever.

## A check whose producer was replaced runs, finds plenty, and is wrong — and the coverage block cannot see it

The deleted-check section below is about a check that lost its *column*, which skips and says so. The opposite case bit on 22 September 2026 and is the harder one: a check whose column kept its name and position while the thing writing it was replaced. Two here did, and between them they produced every one of that capture's 1,089 definitive findings — a report in which eight real findings sat under 1,089 repetitions of two false ones.

```
what the check restated                      what the course brain writes there now
├─ <activity>_offer is the chooser's         the course's three-valued census admission (0.44.0),
│  eligibility, and only a Usable or         with `not-compared` for an activity the course mints
│  Unresolved one may be selected            no domain for — which keeping company always is
└─ choice_id is the comparison identity      DecideCourseEachTick.DecisionId (0.43.0), advancing
   Chooser.Choose advances when it            once per decision *reached*, so an activity change
   replaces the current activity              inside one decision contradicts nothing
```

Both now gate on the capture's declared schema, which is the only witness either has. `SelectedActivitiesHadAnEligibleOffer` exempts `not-compared` from 0.44.0 and keeps the reading that is still a contradiction there — a selected activity whose own domain census read `KnownUnusable` or `NoOpportunity`, which is the course binding a step in a domain it had itself proved unusable. `ARetainedChoiceKeepsItsSelection` skips by name from 0.43.0 rather than being deleted, because every capture on disk from before 21 September carries the chooser's identity and the rule still grades those. **`TheBoundActivityHoldsWhileOneDecisionRuns` is a new class and not that one renamed**, by this folder's own rule that a name in the coverage block is read as the question asked: it counts the activity changes inside one decision identity, once for the whole session, at **Potential** — because a row carries the decision identity and the activity and *not* whether that decision had settled, so it cannot separate a published course legitimately carrying an activity change from the flicker loop, and the finding says which record would. On the 22 September capture it reports 214 transitions, 212 of them `combat→keep-company`, which is the same loop the recorder's own `activity-exited-during-decision` contract names from the producer's side at schema 0.45.0.

## A check can be brought back rather than deleted, and one now has been

`CombatHeldTargetsItsBinderCouldNotSee` was `HuntingHadAWeaponThatCouldReach`, reading the arsenal's bounded shortlist of rejected weapon-target pairs out of `target_evidence`. The weapon block's rename at schema 0.40.0 took that column, and the check was a named skip on every capture for five schema versions while the shortlist it parsed was written nowhere. Schema 0.45.0 gives the two names back with the **course's** meaning, and the class asks the question the 22 September 2026 capture raised instead: a fight held while every target the census admitted read as not observed to the binder that had to use it.

Three properties of that revival are the reusable part. **The subject changed, so the class name changed**, because a name in the coverage block is read as the question asked and "could it reach what it was hunting" is not this one. **A column whose meaning moved under its own name needs a witness**, so `frame_ms` sits in the check's `Needs` unread and a pre-0.40.0 capture — which carries both names holding the arsenal's format — skips rather than being parsed confidently as something else; the self-test asserts that through `Program.Evaluate` rather than by asking the check, because asking the check proves nothing about the runner. And **the predicate parses its entry from the right-hand end**, because the fact key inside it is a `FactKey` whose own rendering carries two colons, so a left-to-right split finds five fields where the format names three. That last one was found by the fixture before its first green run, which is the only moment questioning a test is free.

## Four movement checks went with the walking body, and each would have gone silent rather than red

The orb has no proved edge with a kind, a proven tick count, an outcome and a macro refusal in front of it — it has a route of free-space corners flown by momentum steering — so four checks lost their subject outright. Naming them here is the point: each read a column or an occurrence field the producer no longer writes, which means each would have kept reporting a clean run for ever while every report listed it as covered. That is the failure this folder's coverage block exists to prevent and cannot catch on its own, because a check that runs and finds nothing looks exactly like a check that finds nothing wrong.

- **`ProvenMovesTakeTheirProvenTime`** timed one move against the ticks its simulation proved it would take, from `edge_proven` and `edge_took`.
- **`EveryMoveOfferedGetsMade`** asked whether a kind of move that was offered was ever once completed, from `edge_kind`, `edge_outcome` and `next_kind`. It is the check this folder's own rule about thresholds was written from, and the lesson survives it.
- **`TheTwoBodiesAgree`** reported the distance between the offline motion rule and the collision that performed it, from `diverge` and `diverge_valid`. There is one body and one contact now, run identically in the mod and in every headless tool, so there is no second prediction to disagree with.
- **`PersistentRejectionsAreFindings`** read the navigator's `last-rejection` out of a god's-eye `movement-state` sample. A refusal was the macro proof turning a step down before its first tick; the orb's search either returns a corridor or does not, and the sample no longer carries the field.

**`SubmergedMotionGetsExplained` is deleted and deliberately not replaced.** It watched a stationary body's breath bar run down. The orb has no breath, and every liquid is air to it, so no liquid hurts, slows or holds it and there is no stall of that shape left to watch for. A check written for one now would be written from an imagined failure and would fire on the shape its author imagined.

**What replaced them is narrower and says more.** The engine does not integrate this body, so the mod's own circle contact is the only thing entitled to stop it, and the contact reports itself: `touched_wall`, `wall_normal` and `clearance`. A pinned stretch with no wall touched and clearance well clear of zero is therefore a body nothing in the record accounts for — a stronger statement than the walking body could ever make, because a walker could always be resting on a tile nobody recorded.

**`TheBodyMovesWhenDriven` reads `desired_vel` for "driven", never `npc_vel`, and reversing that is the way to break it silently.** The motor writes `npc.velocity` from the displacement the circle contact allowed, so a body pressed into a wall reads a velocity of almost exactly zero on precisely the ticks this check exists to catch; `desired_vel` is what the brain asked the body to accelerate toward after capping. The fixture in `../Tests/` is built from that record — zero velocity, 2 px a tick desired — and asserts the Definitive grade, so a check reading the wrong column returns Potential and goes red.

**A column can change meaning without changing name, and a check whose arithmetic depends on which meaning names a witness column.** `npc_px` is the live case: under the walker it was an after-helpers compatibility position anchored on the feet, and it is now the orb's centre, which is the point `SuccessRegion.Contains` and `FindToolAccess.InReachBox` are both handed. `ClaimedArrivalsStayInsideTheirSuccessRegion` therefore names `touched_wall` in its `Needs` although it never reads it, so an older capture skips rather than being judged by geometry measured from the wrong point — which it was, once, and it produced a confident Definitive finding on the 13:27 capture before the witness was added.

**Route progress is two signals, because smoothing made the index blind to a straight flight.** `FollowingMakesRouteProgress` resets its watch on a `route_index` rise, which under the walker's tile graph happened every tile. The orb's route is smoothed by skipping raw corners into one segment while the swept body stays clear, so a crossing of open space is a single segment and the index cannot move for its whole duration — a body flying straight at the player would have reported "follow remained unsatisfied without completing a route step" for ever. `route_remaining_px` is the second signal: it is the body's own projection onto its current segment, so it falls only when the body moves, unlike the tick estimate beside it, which falls as time passes whatever the body does. It is credited against the window's start rather than the previous row, because a per-row comparison credits the projection's jitter and turns the check silent, and only while `route_search_id` is unchanged, because a replan can shorten a route the body never travelled. Both halves have a fixture, and each was mutation-run against the rule it replaces.

**Following is admitted against one box.** The region carries its own centre and half-extents from the resolve that admitted the destination, and the request's anchor travels beside it as evidence of what was aimed at rather than as a second acceptance box — it stopped widening acceptance when the region gained its own growth. A check still admitting the anchor passes exactly the arrival the single-box rule was written to catch, so the fixture asserts that a body parked on its anchor and far outside the region *fires*.

The stop rate's denominator is counted from the rows under the producer's own predicate — the ordinary `travel` owner with an `Executable` or `Partial` navigator — and the finding prints the recorder's own running `stops_per_minute` beside it, so the two disagreeing is itself visible rather than silent.
