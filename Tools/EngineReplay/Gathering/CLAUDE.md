# Gathering fixtures

**Every approach question here reads the reach sense, so a scene must flood the region for the world it actually built.** `VerifyOreWork.SetUp` floods before returning and ends with a `Hold` resolve so it hands back no retained destination; a row that then edits terrain or moves a body calls `VerifyOreWork.ResettleReach`, which throws the region away — including the two `ContinueRouteSearch` objects, which `Refresh` would otherwise reuse and refill from tiles it had already expanded through. Priming with "resolve while the region is incomplete" does nothing here, because a stale region is complete.

**A row that produced an undecided approach by starving `AStar.MsBudget` is testing nothing.** That clock no longer reaches the approach at all. Two rows were found still doing it and both now empty the region instead, which is the mechanism that actually produces Unknown: `AnUnprovenApproachIsNotAPlan` and W05's undecided case. `AnUnknownApproachDoesNotSubstituteASealedNeighbour` needs a proven No and an Unknown in one scene, which one flood cannot supply — completeness is a property of the whole flood — so it gets the No from geometry, a sealed ore whose pose scan finds nothing to rank and never consults the region.

Ore jobs, ceiling hops, chopping cooperation and remaining-work accounting. `--ore-work` and `--mining-baseline` select this group.

## Usable ore

P04 runs the whole brain against native collision. A nearer ore sealed on every face and one exposed only inside an unreachable chamber must each yield to an exposed farther ore with the enclosure left whole. A pose exactly on the reach edge must mine without closing in. Four starts on both sides at different sub-tile offsets must each end in a native break with every strike from actual reach, and the suite prints where each first strike came from. Ceiling ore in a two-row slab in both orientations must be discovered through a proven hop and mined from a raised body that then lands, while ore beyond any hop stays unoffered. A pick too weak offers known-unusable work; a stronger pick starts the job on the next preparation; swapping back after real damage ends it partial. A player wall built round an approached ore must end the attempt as failed method with no strike; opening one notch face must let the same ore be mined — that notch is one the game's `CanHitLine` refuses, deliberately. Each case lifts the planning allowances. The suite prints where each first strike came from and prints decide and whole-tick timings on a scene where every ore has proven standing No.

## Mining hops

`VerifyMiningHops` attacks the hop method where the ceiling case cannot. A pit beside the take-off must hold a body arriving at walking speed from either side under native NPC collision, and the whole brain must mine without feet dropping below floor. A body dropped into that pit must stop being offered its old take-off within bounded time and close a failed attempt with no strike. Water poured onto a proven take-off (liquid motion announces no terrain revision) must end the job as `interaction-jump-lost-take-off` and keep that take-off unoffered for the whole watch window. A scan whose planning deadline has already passed must answer Unknown both where the unbounded scan says Yes and where it says No. A tall ceiling needs a pose more than six rows below standing reach, and a ledge take-off must be climbed to, mined from and landed back on. Every case except the deadline one lifts the planning allowances. G1 is a known limitation asserted as it stands: above a pillar top two tiles wide the scan must answer No although a jump from rest reaches the ore, while the same top three tiles wide admits a take-off.

## Gathering cooperation

`VerifyGatheringCooperation` runs P09's cooperation and permission contracts through the whole brain on native tiles. Every case diffs the full tile map so an edit to anything but the work target fails it. Chopping from either side of a trunk with a block beside it must fell it with every strike from actual axe reach and conclude as the companion's own completion. The player hitting the companion's trunk mid-chop must move it to the separate trunk with no further strike on the shared one, end the attempt it left as partial with `player-took-trunk`, and after the player fells the first trunk leave no shared completion behind. A trunk felled externally before any strike is an invalid attempt; after a companion strike a shared completion. Chop departure repeats the mining departure with native remaining hits. A bed placed after approach while walking, in cooldown and during ceiling hop must stop every further strike, release the tool hand and end the attempt partial or invalid; removing it must let work finish. The work policy is switched off or to Mimic with no player contact while walking, in cooldown and during ceiling hop in flight. A player axe hit observed under Mimic, followed by chopping disabled longer than both the watcher's contact memory and the Mimic job window, must leave Mimic waiting for the player rather than reading old contact as recent. An ore the pick cannot damage must never be struck while the whole brain fells the usable tree beside it. A retained mining job must leave a new tree prepared and valued on the same board and must lose to it once the vein is worth nothing. Mining and chopping forecasts, less their native remaining work, must equal the walk in pixels over walking speed.

## Work accounting

`VerifyWorkAccounting` checks what a mining job reports against what the world shows. A two-tile vein the companion clears with its own strikes while the player places a third copper tile touching it must conclude partial as `tracked-portion-clear-vein-continues` and the added tile must become the next job. Equal remaining work is compared across two scenes that differ only in who removed one tile of a two-tile vein, the companion's native strikes or the player, with the companion on the same feet: value, forecast, remaining tiles, target and native remaining hits must be identical, and the remaining count must be one. A third scene adds the player's own partial pick damage and requires the companion's offer unchanged. Work cancelled by range runs under the close distance mode, since an active job's allowance under standard mode is wider than this world can separate player from ore; after one removal the player leaves and no strike may follow — the job must end with two tiles present and one companion removal, and the attempt must be partial.

## Collection contracts

`VerifyCollectionContracts` drives the real collection activity and the real bag transfer path. A drop at the bottom of a sealed pit must be a known-unusable offer both with one-way drops refused (unreachable) and allowed (would strand the companion), while a drop on open floor stays usable. A drop that rolls fourteen tiles after preparation must make execution hold; the next preparation must walk to where it lies. A stack of fifty valuable bars valued with cargo room for fifty and for two must be worth less in the second; taking the two through `Collect` must end the attempt partial, and the full cargo must refuse the remainder for capacity. A drop the bag took whole concludes as the companion's own completion; one the player took with nothing transferred as invalid; one split between them as shared; a drop already taken by contact pickup leaves no offer. A floor drop's forecast must be no more than the straight walk to it over walking speed and within four tiles of it. Four drops at the bottom of a sealed pit, every one nearer than a reachable floor drop, must not hide it: the floor drop is offered on the **first** preparation, however many refused drops lie nearer. That row used to assert a bound on how many preparations the delay could take, which made the delay part of the contract; the delay is gone rather than shortened, so the row asserts its absence, and a reinstated three-drop budget turns it red on `KnownUnusable/drop-unreachable`. Each case lifts the planning allowances and turns pot breaking off, so the known-drop method is the only one compared. The suite prints NearbyAssistance preparation timings from whole-brain ticks under production allowances while four drops fall on open floor or into that pit and a reachable drop appears once they settle.
