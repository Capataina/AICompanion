# Historical evidence distinguishes decisions from outcomes

This folder reconstructs how the companion reached its present design. Commits record what an attempt claimed; conversations record the requirements and interpretations that prompted it; captures and source establish narrower observable facts. Agreement between copies of the same account is not independent corroboration.

Keep private conversation identifiers, raw transcripts and personal material outside the repository. Cite a dated conversation description, its relevant subject and an exact repository commit where available. Record a local retrieval procedure without publishing session identifiers. A historical claim remains historical unless its stated check was repeated.

The investigation covers the pre-utility implementation, subsequent architecture decisions, regressions, abandoned alternatives and the motives behind diagnostic instruments. Its documents are evidence for the proposals, not implementation instructions.
