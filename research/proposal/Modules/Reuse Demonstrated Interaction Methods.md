# Reuse demonstrated methods when repeated protocols create duplicated work

The [core plan](<../Implement the Retained Course Brain.md>) has explicit local interaction phases and generic course repair. It does not need a hand-authored global behaviour tree. This optional extension addresses several real domains repeatedly reconstructing the same multi-step protocol with the same resource and cancellation semantics.

**Activation evidence:** at least three concrete shipped consumers share the protocol, duplicate phase logic or repeated binding cost is measurable, and their preconditions/effects can be expressed without naming a scenario such as “always kill the slime before lighting.” A single intricate weapon is still its own local mechanism.

Extract a reusable method that expands into ordinary `StepBinding` records with parameters, preconditions, resource phases and effects. Its output competes under the existing course policy. Method order never becomes category priority, method completion never mandates resumption, and learned/reused examples must be revalidated against current capabilities and world facts.

| Benefit | Cost and failure trigger | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| One tested cancellation/resource protocol replaces duplicated execution logic. | A generic method can hide domain-specific native preconditions. | All original consumers retain native-effect and interruption fixtures, including changed gear and external removal. |
| Reusing a valid expansion can reduce repeated construction. | Cached bindings become stale under different worlds or capabilities. | Rebind parameters and dependencies before acceptance; measured construction cost falls without worse choices. |

Telemetry records method definition/revision, expansion inputs, concrete generated bindings and the first failed precondition. The definition is data about how an action may be performed, not a second owner choosing what to do. Mutate capability, target generation and resource phase; no old accepted expansion may execute. Remove the abstraction when only one consumer remains or callers need enough exceptions that their shared contract is fictional.
