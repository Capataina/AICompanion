Since then this folder is the design the code is measured against rather than a design waiting to be built: the root `CLAUDE.md`'s current-state section says what of it exists.

**A second audit landed on 22 September 2026, after the first play, and closed one of the four gates the first audit found with no fixture row at all.** G13, encounter conduct, now has three arms proven by fixture and mutation: an encounter suppresses a whole optional course while leaving a fight worth what it was; among two feasible fights the survivable one wins, and only while the encounter is on (the `Courses/CLAUDE.md` survival-first section carries the idle-course hole this arm closed); and the player's death leaves the encounter standing (the arm a sentinel found the plan's own death clause had no detector for anywhere in the tree, proven by planting `if (player.dead) { ... return; }` at the top of `EncounterSense.Update` and watching it redden). **G10, no activity or interaction builds a spatial fact the senses already publish, still does not cover its gate**: it is a whole-identifier match now rather than a substring one, after a sentinel showed three of its six refused patterns were spelled from filenames and matched nothing, but two plan mutations are named in the guide as surviving it structurally, and the third gate the second audit named is unbuilt with the one line that would close most of it. G01a and G06 still have no fixture row. The plan's step-5 cheap continuation and its step-7 comparison instruments (reactive reference, exact oracle, seeded sequences, snapshot replay) are still never written. Writing a plan does not implement it, and a class a fixture exercises and production never calls does not either.

Each roadmap begins by checking the observer and reproducing a failure while separating choice, destination and body execution. It then branches on the measured result. More elaborate planning is justified by a demonstrated need for foresight; an A* replacement is justified by evidence about search work on an adequate movement model. Neither is a remedy inferred from the appearance of a stalled companion alone.

## Findings that constrain implementation

Retaining an activity name cannot order concrete torch/drop/enemy targets. Predicted effects are not native receipts. A future's value must be evaluated after the preceding effects rather than summing independent benefits. Unknown reach is not absence. A local tactical planner may refine an accepted combat binding, but cannot secretly perform a different action. A nominal search deadline does not bound setup, held-plan reevaluation or fallback work.

The first-version numerical preference policy is an explicit design hypothesis constrained by the README contrasts. Its central function and tests own corrections; private activity bonuses and category priorities would recreate the architecture this plan replaces. The live executor, course owner and native body each have one authority.
