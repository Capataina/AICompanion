# Concrete observed work enters one fair discovery surface

Domain sources expose individual targets with stable purpose, target and generation keys. A changing pose is new evidence about an existing opportunity, not a new purpose. Admission remains tri-state. The shared binder requires a complete tracked-input manifest and an executable physical prefix; only the domain knows the native conditions for its next use.

**"Fair" in the heading means fair on two axes, and only one of them held until 21 September 2026.** Discovery has always been fair on *turns*: sources round-robin under a shared allowance, so a tiny slice still examines every source and no source can spend the whole budget. It was not fair on *survival*. The candidates every source produced went into one bounded store of sixty-four with a plain recency eviction, so the domain that mints the most simply kept it: measured in a dark area, lighting minted 138 sites while mining, chopping and collection minted one candidate each, and all three of those were evicted while lighting held all sixty-four. The companion lit and could not discover a vein with the ore in front of it, a pickaxe in its slot and the policy permitting — and nothing was red anywhere, because a domain with no surviving candidate reports no admission group rather than a refusal, so every count a reader would check was simply absent.

The store is grouped by domain now, with every source guaranteed a floor of `Capacity / sources` and only what it holds above that floor competing on recency. The floor is derived from the capacity the caller already chose rather than tuned, and it is divided by the number of *registered* sources rather than the number currently producing, because sizing it to who has arrived lets the first arrival claim everything. `DecisionStorage` owns the rule, so any other bounded store that shares one capacity between several producers inherits it by passing a grouping.

**The eviction count that names a starved domain was itself unreadable until 21 September 2026, which is why AIC-448 took four eliminated hypotheses to find.** `DiscoverOpportunities.Continue` overwrote `coverage[source]` with each slice's fresh coverage, and the eviction handler accumulates into that same row — so the count the handler had just written was reset on every slice, and `Evicted` read zero however many candidates the store had thrown away. A source reports what it examined and cannot know what happened to it afterwards, so the slice's row carries the domain's running eviction total forward, per world epoch like the store itself. The general shape: **a counter written by one owner and a row overwritten by another cannot share a field**, and the tell is a diagnostic that reads zero in exactly the scene it was built for.

The property is worth stating as a property, because the family chooser this replaced had it under another name — `ScheduleOpportunityQueries`' per-family preparation share — and the course lost it in the migration rather than deciding against it: **a shared bounded resource divided by recency alone is a preference for the loudest producer, and every producer needs a floor it cannot be pushed below.** `VerifyCourseCore`'s `G03 a prolific discovery source cannot starve a quiet one out of the store` is the standing guard, written at the store rather than at the scene that found it so a domain added later inherits it.

├─ DescribeOpportunity.cs    keys, physical needs, census units and source/binder interfaces
├─ DiscoverOpportunities.cs  round-robin resumable discovery with cache coverage
├─ DiscoverAssistanceOpportunities.cs pure drop, light and pot census adapters

The census owns normalisation; enumeration order, cache occupancy and target partition do not own value. Sources report examined bounds, unfinished work and loss of cached coverage. Changed revisions dirty estimates and invoke validation; they are not themselves proof that a native action became forbidden. Bind/predict/compare consumers read immutable snapshots and sparse hypothetical state, never `Main`.

**Domain migration is finished and this folder is the live discovery surface**, since `0bb2c8a` on 21 September 2026 put `DecideCourseEachTick` on the brain tick. The sentence that stood here until then — that the presence of this folder was not a replacement of family selection — described the tree before the switch and had stopped being true. Six sources and six binders are registered, and the count in this file means domains rather than jobs:

```
domain           source                            binder
mine-target      GatheringOpportunitySource        GatheringOpportunityBinder
chop-target      GatheringOpportunitySource        GatheringOpportunityBinder
collect-target   DiscoverAssistanceOpportunities   AssistanceOpportunityBinder
light-target     DiscoverAssistanceOpportunities   AssistanceOpportunityBinder
pot-target       DiscoverAssistanceOpportunities   AssistanceOpportunityBinder
combat           CombatOpportunitySource           CombatOpportunityBinder
```

Combat's pair lives with the combat stance rather than here, because a use is minted per shot. **Domains and activities are two sets that nearly coincide, and reading one count as the other is the mistake this table exists to stop.** Keeping company is an activity with no domain at all, deliberately — an empty order *is* companionship, with its own projected costs, so there is nothing to discover and nothing to bind — while a pot is a domain with no activity, broken in passing by whatever activity is already travelling. `../ExecuteCourseBinding.cs` writes the mapping out by hand rather than deriving it from the domain string for exactly this reason.

A pending binding exposes its missing native travel as typed requests. The shared gate requires every requested key to appear as missing in that invocation's tracked reads, and forbids requests alongside a completed binding. A concluded unresolved answer is not a pending request: repeating it would consume the shared allowance without adding evidence.

`DiscoverAssistanceOpportunities` reads `collect-target`, `light-target` and `pot-target` facts from immutable snapshots and resumes discovery under a borrowed allowance. `CaptureAssistanceOpportunities` owns the native reads. Its drop census freezes item content before slicing contact work; target generations distinguish observed replacements, while replacement between observations remains unknown. The pot and light capture paths remain the least-exercised geometry here: both publish coverage and both bind, but neither has been watched in play under the course.

**A source calls its census exhausted only when it reads a `<domain>-coverage` fact as `Observed`, and until 21 September 2026 the capture published that fact for drops alone.** Light and pot sites were captured with no coverage beside them, so those two domains could never report a finished census — and under this tree's standing rule that optional work does not start on an unanswered search, a live brain reading them would never have placed a torch or broken a pot. Neither half was wrong against its own fixture, which is exactly why it survived: the sources were tested against facts written by hand, the capture was tested on what it emitted, and nothing ran the two together. The pot and light scans sweep a fixed window with no cursor and no borrowed allowance, so unlike the drop census they cannot stop part-way and their coverage is honestly `Observed`; both now publish it with the swept area, so a reader can tell which world a complete answer is complete about. The standing guard is `G03 every assistance domain's real capture feeds its own discovery` in `Tools/EngineReplay/Assistance/VerifyAssistanceOpportunityDiscovery.cs`, which drives the real capture into the three real sources and asserts exhaustion rather than a site count, because an empty floor legitimately has nothing to find and only the coverage answer separates *looked and found nothing* from *never finished looking*.

The durable property, which outlives these three domains: **two halves of one seam that are each only ever tested against a hand-written stand-in for the other will agree with their fixtures and not with each other.** A seam earns one row that runs both real halves together.

**Every domain with a source now has a binder, where until 21 September 2026 three of them had none.** Collect, light and pot were discovered, enumerated and priced and could never produce a `StepBinding`, so no course could contain them — the same silence one layer up from the missing coverage facts, and the reason a live tick switch would have given a companion that mines, chops and fights and never lights, loots or breaks a pot. `AssistanceOpportunityBinder` is one class taking its domain rather than three, because the three differ only in what the hand does on arrival and which need they satisfy, and a shared binder keeps the travel gate, the census gate and the identity checks from drifting apart between them. It checks the census fact itself rather than trusting discovery: a source deciding its own enumeration finished says nothing about whether *this* site was observed under a complete census, and a site bound from a partial one is work chosen against a world nobody finished looking at. A pickup reserves the body and not the hand — a drop is taken by contact as the body arrives, and reserving a hand it never uses would refuse an opportunistic shot on the way past — while a torch and a pot are a use the hand performs on arrival. A moved drop retires the *application* and not the opportunity, because the contact pose is part of the binding and the drop is still worth taking from wherever it rolled to.

`Tools/EngineReplay/Assistance/VerifyAssistanceCourseBindings.cs` holds those five properties, and the binder-coverage row in `Tools/EngineReplay/DecisionMaking/VerifyCourseBindingExecution.cs` now guards every sourced domain rather than carrying three of them on a pending list.
