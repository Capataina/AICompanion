# Concrete observed work enters one fair discovery surface

Domain sources expose individual targets with stable purpose, target and generation keys. A changing pose is new evidence about an existing opportunity, not a new purpose. Admission remains tri-state. The shared binder requires a complete tracked-input manifest and an executable physical prefix; only the domain knows the native conditions for its next use.

```text
Opportunities/
├─ CLAUDE.md                  source and binding contracts
├─ DescribeOpportunity.cs    keys, physical needs, census units and source/binder interfaces
├─ DiscoverOpportunities.cs  round-robin resumable discovery with cache coverage
├─ DiscoverAssistanceOpportunities.cs pure drop, light and pot census adapters
└─ BindOpportunity.cs        shared manifest/epoch gate and domain live validation
```

The census owns normalisation; enumeration order, cache occupancy and target partition do not own value. Sources report examined bounds, unfinished work and loss of cached coverage. Changed revisions dirty estimates and invoke validation; they are not themselves proof that a native action became forbidden. Bind/predict/compare consumers read immutable snapshots and sparse hypothetical state, never `Main`.

Domain migration remains in progress; until all adapters and the coordinator use these contracts, the presence of this folder is not a replacement of family selection.

A pending binding exposes its missing native travel as typed requests. The shared gate requires every requested key to appear as missing in that invocation's tracked reads, and forbids requests alongside a completed binding. A concluded unresolved answer is not a pending request: repeating it would consume the shared allowance without adding evidence.

`DiscoverAssistanceOpportunities` reads `collect-target`, `light-target` and `pot-target` facts from immutable snapshots and resumes discovery under a borrowed allowance. `CaptureAssistanceOpportunities` owns the native reads. Its drop census freezes item content before slicing contact work; target generations distinguish observed replacements, while replacement between observations remains unknown. The pot and light capture paths are still provisional in their geometry and admission, so they do not yet establish the full native course-binding contract.

**A source calls its census exhausted only when it reads a `<domain>-coverage` fact as `Observed`, and until 21 September 2026 the capture published that fact for drops alone.** Light and pot sites were captured with no coverage beside them, so those two domains could never report a finished census — and under this tree's standing rule that optional work does not start on an unanswered search, a live brain reading them would never have placed a torch or broken a pot. Neither half was wrong against its own fixture, which is exactly why it survived: the sources were tested against facts written by hand, the capture was tested on what it emitted, and nothing ran the two together. The pot and light scans sweep a fixed window with no cursor and no borrowed allowance, so unlike the drop census they cannot stop part-way and their coverage is honestly `Observed`; both now publish it with the swept area, so a reader can tell which world a complete answer is complete about. The standing guard is `G03 every assistance domain's real capture feeds its own discovery` in `Tools/EngineReplay/Assistance/VerifyAssistanceOpportunityDiscovery.cs`, which drives the real capture into the three real sources and asserts exhaustion rather than a site count, because an empty floor legitimately has nothing to find and only the coverage answer separates *looked and found nothing* from *never finished looking*.

The durable property, which outlives these three domains: **two halves of one seam that are each only ever tested against a hand-written stand-in for the other will agree with their fixtures and not with each other.** A seam earns one row that runs both real halves together.
