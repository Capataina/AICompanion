# Behaviour selection — utility scoring

`ChooseBehaviour.cs` prepares every activity and evaluates its captured value through the shared factor machinery. Gathering, Combat and NearbyAssistance each nominate their highest positive-value child; the parent compares those concrete nominations without another weight or commitment bonus. `LastScores` and `LastNominations` retain the child ledger and family results for diagnostics. Selection returns the chosen executor; that executor supplies a PositionRequest to position selection. The chooser neither selects a tile nor controls the body.

**Hunting outranks work by being eligible, never by outscoring it, so a discount of hunt against work would change nothing.** Across the 2026-09-11 session, mining won 1,368 of the 1,734 ticks where it had a live target and torch placement won the other 366; hunting won none of them. Hunting reached 32.2% of that session because it was eligible three-quarters of the time and everything better was absent. The lever is therefore whatever makes useful work *eligible* — an ore whose approach the search could not decide, a target admitted with no way to shoot it — and never the distance between two ceilings. Lowering hunt's constant buys the appearance of a priority ruling and restores the behaviour in the next crowded cave, which is why these measurements are recorded here instead.

Hunting seeks an attack opportunity. Useful investigation belongs to lighting, collecting or other concrete work, while idle local movement belongs to keeping company in the agreed final structure. Hunting does not require an independent exploration purpose to justify its existence.

Regroup time uses available route-step durations and the positioner's observed travel estimate as well as straight-line distance. Excursions are discounted by protection urgency, and that is the only place the player's danger reaches optional work: urgency already compares each threat's arrival with the companion's intervention time, so an activity that also multiplied its own raw value by player danger charged one threat twice and could not tell a shot it can take from across the room from one it cannot. Raw values read the companion's own danger where it matters to that activity (hunting's own-skin term) and never the player's. `VerifyCompanionActivities` holds the four matched scenes — nobody, the player, the companion, both threatened — that pin this, with a real opportunity for every excursion in each scene so no raw value can hide a danger term behind a zero. Their own acquisition and continuation bounds determine whether distance permits the job. Charging them again for leaving the short follow-comfort band would defeat that permission. Interruptible work pays the time until it can yield, rather than the duration of its entire job, against the threat horizon. Unknown intervention is conservative; the companion remains autonomous while the player is dead, with player-protection pressure removed.

A boss fight or world event reaches optional work through the same protection factor and nowhere else. `ActivityComparisonContext.EncounterIntensity` carries the observation's intensity, and a non-combat excursion — mining, chopping, collecting, lighting — pays `1 − max(urgency, intensity)`, while a candidate marked `ServesEncounter` (the chooser marks the Combat family) pays urgency alone. The maximum rather than the product is the point: a boss is also a threat that raises urgency and crowd pressure is the threats themselves, so multiplying the two would charge one danger twice. Keeping company is not an excursion and is untouched, which is deliberate rather than an omission, because nomination selects only positive values and zeroing every non-combat child would leave a quiet blood-moon lull with no activity at all; the README's wider encounter leash belongs to position and movement, not to this factor. Stranded companions pay neither term. `VerifyEncounterContext` pins the arithmetic and a live mining scene that stops only when the event reaches the player.

## Preparation is scheduled per family, so one expensive family cannot spend the tick

`ScheduleOpportunityQueries` decides which activities prepare in a comparison. Families run in enum order; inside each, the incumbent and every non-excursion child (protection and keeping company) always prepare, then optional children prepare in a rotating order. The first optional child always runs, and each optional child runs inside a `LimitPlanningWork.Narrow` deadline set to what remains of the family's share (`Weights.FamilyPreparationMilliseconds`); further optional children run only while that share is unspent. Because every nested route, reach and approach query reads the shared deadline, the share bounds an optional child's whole preparation, not just whether its siblings start. Mandatory children deliberately run under the tick's total allowance instead: tool-approach queries are fresh bounded A* searches per candidate pose with no retained progress, so narrowing the incumbent would turn an approach the body is already walking into the same undecided answer every tick. A skipped child is written to the board as `Deferred` with zero value and the reason `family-preparation-allowance-spent`; its retained discovery is neither compared nor allowed to win. The family's next comparison starts from the first child it deferred, so a family of n optional children prepares each at least once every n comparisons. `Queries.LastFamilies` carries prepared and deferred counts and milliseconds per family for the recorder and harness.

This is scheduling, not preference: deferral never lowers a prepared value, and protection, keeping company and the running activity cannot be starved by it. The share exists so optional siblings cannot pile their own searches onto a tick: the brain-cost scene measured a single incumbent mining approach recomputation spending the whole total planning allowance, and anything prepared after it on that tick would have found nothing left. That incumbent cost was a property of the approach query itself, not of scheduling, and it was fixed there: the tool-access query now asks nearest poses first and stops at the first reachable one, which brought the same scene's worst decide tick to about 4 ms. With `LimitPlanningWork.Unbounded` set nothing is deferred, which keeps offline fixtures deterministic; a fixture that tests the rotation sets the chooser's share to zero, which deterministically prepares exactly one optional child per family.

## The urgency ladder, and why an action topping out at one can never interrupt anything

Candidate preparation and shared comparison are separate calls. The chooser invokes each adapter's Prepare once, then reads its captured raw value, forecast and classification through context-free methods. `EvaluatePreparedActivities` accepts only immutable scalar records, reads no live activity or world object and never invokes discovery. It returns all factors and an explicit error for invalid or non-finite values. Positive-infinite threat horizon means no observed deadline. Repeated or reordered evaluation preserves each candidate's value. Preparation still owns mutable discovery caches; execution ownership is handled separately below.

Incumbent commitment is deliberately independent of the shared body-stall flag. A replacement activity would otherwise inherit its predecessor's physical failure before attempting any movement. Activity-owned remaining effort and outcomes must replace that coarse commitment; body-level stalling alone cannot decide that a job was a bad choice.

Prepare is required for every adapter rather than an optional hook. Score and ForecastTicks receive no world context. All ordinary candidates obey that boundary. Keeping company combines reunion and relaxed movement in one candidate. Environmental escape and combat spacing run through SharedSafety rather than competing as candidates.

Zero and invalid candidates produce no family nomination. If all families are empty, selection returns no activity and releases the former one; the coordinator holds ordinary movement while independent hands and earlier safety/recovery paths retain their existing contracts. Positive candidates use one tie key at both levels: their stable registration index. Group order and duplicated identical candidates cannot change the result. The flat selector exists only as a test reference.

Each prepared candidate also captures its target binding. Before activation, the chooser verifies that the adapter still names that identity, that an NPC remains alive/active in the same spawn generation, or that an item still has quantity and retains its type. A rejected candidate keeps its original raw value and named rejection in the ledger but loses selectable value. Families nominate again from the remaining prepared board without discovery. Shared opportunity costs are recomputed from captured values so a rejected work offer cannot keep suppressing companionship. Each rejection removes one candidate, bounding reconsideration by the board size.

Item activation also verifies that the captured object still occupies its world slot, naming a replaced slot separately from exhausted quantity or a changed type. Same-type replacement cannot inherit the old offer. This binding check does not establish native tile permission, a useful arrival pose, or a new generation when an arbitrary mod mutates the same item object in place without a lifecycle signal. Those remain the activity-specific offer and native interaction contracts; non-entity identities currently use the adapter's existing equality semantics. It also does not make an unresolved route proven merely because its target still exists.

Before comparing the captured identity, activation also reads the activity's side-effect-free prepared-target rejection. Tile work uses this to distinguish a vanished tile from changed material, preserving the original raw value and letting families nominate another prepared candidate. Coordinate/material binding cannot detect an unobserved same-material replacement and does not certify tool permission or access.

Gathering's prepared validation also rejects a disabled work policy. This is live permission evidence, separate from the previously captured utility value: an unchanged ore or tree cannot keep an offer selectable after its work permission is revoked. Native mutation permission and execution cleanup remain with the activity and tool.

After identity validation, a nominated attack activity exposes its captured PositionRequest for lazy method admission through the existing positioner. No established firing destination removes that candidate's selectable value with a method-specific reason while retaining its raw desirability and tested alternatives. The families nominate again from the prepared board; this does not rerun opportunity discovery or lower the perceived threat. Failed method admission is bounded evidence, never a permanent unreachable judgement. A later comparison can refine the method again or admit an opened shot. The score ledger retains method evidence for accepted and rejected queries; candidates never queried have no invented method evidence.

Mine/chop belong to Gathering; guard/hunt belong to Combat; collection, torches and keeping company belong to NearbyAssistance. Keeping company prepares the greater value of reunion and relaxed local companionship, then pays the shared useful-work discount once; those methods do not receive separate incumbent bonuses or activity identities. Collection compares its known-drop and potential-content offers using the same ComparisonContext and evaluator, then publishes the chosen raw offer for parent evaluation. The parent does not multiply an already-discounted method score. SharedSafety owns escape, collision avoidance and combat spacing outside these nominations. Richer unresolved-method eligibility remains separate offer-contract work.

`OwnCurrentActivity` is the sole holder of the current executor. The chooser delegates activation to it rather than maintaining another current-action field. The owner identifies consecutive purposes using the adapter's activity identity and the NPC spawn generation where that identity is an NPC. Other adapter identities retain their existing value/reference semantics; this does not certify item-slot generation or full offer revalidation. Selecting a different purpose changes the activity ID even when the executor is unchanged. A suspended purpose keeps its ID if freshly selected again; replacement ends it without calling its physical release twice. The owner retains only the current purpose and the last termination summary, not a stack of intentions.

The owner also separates purpose from attempt. Each continuous stretch of execution opens one attempt with its own identity when execution begins and calls the activity's `BeginAttempt`; reselecting the executing purpose keeps it open, suspension closes it as Interrupted with the suspension reason, and replacement or an empty selection closes it with the replaced activity's own `ConcludeAttempt` before Exit runs. Attempt identities come from one process-wide counter rather than one per owner, so a respawned companion's new brain cannot reuse identities a recorder cursor has already written this session. Resuming a suspended purpose therefore keeps the activity identity and opens a fresh attempt with no inherited effects. Productive effects are credited only through `Chooser.RecordWork`, which every caller invokes for an observed productive native effect, and only while an attempt is open, so an effect outside execution belongs to nobody. A bounded list of recent outcomes survives so two conclusions inside one tick — a replacement then a recovery interruption — both reach the recorder. Attempt identity is not a route attempt: the navigator keeps its own.

Prepared candidates carry the activity's offer eligibility into evaluation, and the evaluator returns `value-without-eligible-offer` for positive value beside anything but a usable or unresolved offer. Method admission refines the ledger row it queries: an established destination makes the offer usable for that comparison, none makes it known-unusable with the method reason, and neither blacklists the target for later comparisons.

Repeated selection of the same executing purpose does not create a lifecycle transition. The transient selection stage inside a tick must not refresh its changed-at timestamp or flood the sparse recorder with fictional transitions. Comparison freshness remains separately measurable.

The activity owner also delivers post-control outcome observations only while the ordinary activity is executing. Shared safety can keep observing body progress while that activity is suspended; the resulting elapsed ticks must not consume its failure budget. Resumption restores ordinary observation, so an interrupted unproductive pursuit can still expire after it actually gets control back.

Reflex, recovery and downing explicitly suspend through this owner. Suspension invokes the adapter's release hook once and preserves the remaining purpose's admission identity; reselection prepares opportunities again and re-enters before execution. Moving to another executor releases the previous admission. The legacy discovery caches still have their own retained target data and are not an independent execution owner. A suspension reason is not a failed route or proof that choosing the work was wrong.

Completed comparisons carry an increasing evaluation identity and their engine tick. The coordinator separately records whether selection ran in its current invocation: an early recovery or reflex return can update the brain without refreshing the choice. Retained scores keep their original identity and source time. These comparison identities describe decisions, not ongoing job identity or route attempts.

An attack-method query records an occurrence against the upcoming comparison identity before activation. This preserves rejected alternatives even when the chosen activity stays unchanged between periodic decision snapshots. Its pre-activation phase must not be interpreted as proof that the comparison completed or the activity executed.

Each retained score carries the actual protection, incumbent commitment, horizon and useful-work multipliers applied by this stage. Sparse decision events expose those factors beside raw and final utility; recording consumes the evaluated factors rather than preparing another board, which could acquire or release jobs.

Regrouping raises keeping company's reunion utility rather than introducing a behaviour. It uses the selected distance mode's comfort region, and drops to zero when both axes and local sight satisfy arrival. A viable targeted excursion discounts ordinary companionship inside its permitted activity envelope; protection is unaffected. `IsExcursion` defaults on, with protection and keeping company opting out. Shared safety does not depend on these factors. `RegroupUrgency` and `EstimatedReturnTicks` remain available to diagnostics.

The moving-away component consumes the observation layer's confidence-weighted travel estimate rather than instantaneous velocity. Repeated local motion therefore cannot imply a sustained departing journey solely because its current leg points away. This does not yet replace every activity's distance envelope or establish a useful future meeting region.

Optional activities pay a shared reunion factor based on the additional time their prepared forecast would keep the companion occupied. The cost rate combines observed departure, estimated current return duration and accumulated observed separation. A longer return amplifies the consequence of a departing player; accumulated separation discourages a succession of small detours from becoming permanent absence. Self-danger is not an input, and protection is not an optional excursion. Dead-player and known stranded contexts remove this reunion charge. Existing protection and interruption-horizon factors remain distinct.

The factor is reciprocal in added delay: zero cost preserves the candidate's value, longer work reduces it continuously, and a quick completion can still beat companionship. Only work that remains useful after this charge can discount following. The rate and separation timescale live in BehaviourWeights; matched native work/departure tests calibrate their initial relationship rather than asserting an unconditional gather-versus-follow order.

Separation observation runs after senses even when recovery or shared safety skips ordinary comparison. The chooser's direct entry also observes idempotently for callers outside the coordinator. It counts consecutive observed engine ticks, resets at actual local reunion or player death, and retains accumulated evidence without charging missing intervals. Activity replacement cannot reset that history. Delay rate belongs to the last ordinary comparison and must be read with its freshness. Return duration combines available route evidence with geometric estimates; route evidence sums step durations that describe walking at the walk speed, and it is still not proof of a future return route. Where reunion aims is position selection's meeting place, not this estimate.

The selected job earns continuation by identity on every winning tick, including consecutive targets inside one behaviour. A moving enemy or drop keeps its identity as its position changes; a different entity must pass acquisition again. Mining retains a vein id, chopping a trunk coordinate and material, and small world interactions a target tile. Successful work also records a short-lived collection site so the resulting drops can be gathered under the same ongoing allowance. The shared bounds check both the companion and its target against the player.

An ordinary action scores a product of considerations in 0..1, and the chooser multiplies the incumbent by `Weights.Commitment`. So a running action does not sit at its own score, it sits above it, and an action that has to *interrupt* one has to clear that product rather than merely reach the top of the band. An action whose raw expression cannot exceed 1 therefore loses to a committed ordinary action by construction, on every tick, at every input — not narrowly and not on a weight. Guard was that action, which is why a following companion stood holding a torch with the player's danger reading full, and why turning the danger term up was never going to move it.

`Weights.GuardUrgency` can clear a committed ordinary action, including its incumbent bonus. Its value lives in `BehaviourWeights.cs`. Environmental safety has its own response lifecycle and does not need a larger utility ceiling. New ordinary activities still use comparable current value rather than a fixed ordering among family names.

`Tools/EngineReplay/Movement/VerifyFollowRecoveryAndProtection.cs` exercises immediate guard entry against committed following and continued protection through a small retreat. Native escape fixtures separately exercise environmental response with and without ordinary offers. Those checks establish their inputs rather than every combination of threat and action factors.

Combat, gathering and nearby-assistance activities live in their respective PurposeFamilies folders. Each explicitly declares its purpose family. A new activity must supply that declaration and a preparation implementation; folder names alone do not determine selection. The data flow is observation → preparation → shared evaluation → family nominations → parent choice → activity execution → position selection → shared movement → motor. Movement outcomes return as observed facts rather than a lower stage changing the parent's selected purpose.
