# Shared safety retains escape independently of ordinary work

Safety runs after current danger observation and before ordinary activity selection. It can suspend any activity or act with no ordinary offers. It submits one request to ActivityCoordination; it never applies the motor itself or chooses a weapon.

```
SharedSafety/
├─ CLAUDE.md                   ownership, release conditions and limits
├─ ChooseSafetyResponse.cs     response identity, interruption and common movement requests
├─ ReachEnvironmentalSafety.cs native breathing/lava escape and stable air/landing guidance
└─ CreateCombatSpace.cs        body-state search towards a stable lower-exposure landing
```

Environmental response starts for lava exposure, depleted breathing reserve or an optimistic escape estimate that no longer fits remaining breath. Healthy ordinary water travel remains possible. The estimate is geometric guidance rather than a proven escape duration. Once escape starts, it retains control through emergence to a clear landing; reaching air at a jump apex does not complete it. Collision avoidance retains its response through an airborne aftermath and releases on a grounded observation without imminent collision. Downing and recovery explicitly cancel safety ownership.

Movement owns every candidate control, actual-entry check, unfinished frontier and retained prefix. Environmental escape uses the same current collision forecast as ordinary movement, so it cannot treat a known incoming projectile as absent merely because it is seeking air. If no safe prefix is established, the response reports that failure and retains its purpose; it does not claim a successful escape or downgrade the value of interrupted work.

A replacement safety kind clears the shared body's retained state search. Its frontier and control suffix were proved for the previous response's terminal condition and cannot be inherited as evidence for a different escape. Repeating the same response retains unfinished work.

The air target stays fixed while the body-state search progresses. Native head collision accounts for partial liquid height; body-sized passage distances guide the search around overhangs, while the landing objective remains separate from the first breathing position. Relevant terrain changes invalidate retained geometry. Capability limits come from the motor and shared movement rather than granting unearned swimming, flight or extra jumps.

Landing guidance uses passage distance too: the nearest dry floor by direct distance can be across a wall. After the first breathing position, brief re-submersion preserves the landing phase. Reverting to the nearest air pocket on every dip can refill breath indefinitely while discarding every unfinished shore search; the mirrored awning trace exposed that loop. A changed target's geometry is still revalidated within the retained phase.

Combat spacing starts from the companion's own threat assessment and geometric exposure, independent of player danger and ordinary family offers. It searches through the common body-state controller for stable ground with lower accepted enemy exposure, a breathable head and no lava contact. The response retains its identity through movement and landing, suspends ordinary work, and grants an available hand. Environmental escape and imminent collision can replace it. A completed search without a safe prefix ends explicitly and yields ordinary activity for a bounded retry interval; it does not manufacture successful retreat or failed ordinary work. Existing safety response identity, kind, reason and control grants expose the response in recording.

The admission threshold uses the existing companion-danger estimate, whose damage/time proxies are not a calibrated prediction of effective health loss. The landing exposure threshold and search guidance live in BehaviourWeights. This owner does not establish calibrated tolerable-damage trade-offs, complete multi-hazard consequence prediction or a universal breath-feasible route. The captured-pool and awning fixtures establish their native geometry; wider combined-threat outcomes require their own evidence.

The three responses compose rather than replace each other's purpose, and `Tools/EngineReplay/Combat/VerifySafetyAftermath.cs` holds the evidence. A wounded companion taking combat spacing still fires from its granted hands on ticks the retreat owns the feet. A projectile reflex suspends a guarding activity and the same activity identity resumes when the shot is gone. Guarding reaches the player past an intervening hostile without its hitbox ever meeting that hostile's.

The escape does not price breath against damage. Every escape state is vetoed where a predicted projectile box meets the body, so a hostile shot hanging over the only exit is treated as absolutely impassable rather than as a cost to weigh against drowning. Measured on 2026-09-13 with the captured pool and a stationary shot placed where the unpressured escape first breaks the surface, with planning allowances lifted so the result repeats on any machine: sustained air arrived 38 ticks later and with 12 less life (headless shots deal no damage, so that loss is drowning), passing through `no-safe-prefix` on the way. With the live tick's wall-clock allowances in force the same scene does not repeat: in an otherwise idle process it cost 87 ticks and 26 life, more than a single wooden-arrow hit would have, and in processes sharing the machine with other work the escape found no safe prefix before breath ran out and the companion went down. In play the allowances are always on, so the loaded run is the closer picture of a busy frame; it is not evidence of a live drowning until a recording shows one. The fixture runs lifted and asserts only that the escape still surfaces with life left; a priced trade between hit damage, breath and the aftermath is the open part of Proposal 1's P08, and it needs the movement system's veto to become a cost rather than a predicate.

Collision avoidance's movement is not a dodge. `CoordinateMovement.AvoidThreats` walks toward the player through the veto predicate and never considers a jump, so an arrow at body height on dry floor still lands; `--dodge-repro` exits 1 while that holds. The movement lives in the shared movement system, which owns the fix; this folder only chooses when the reflex responds.
