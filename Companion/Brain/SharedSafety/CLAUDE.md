# Shared safety retains escape independently of ordinary work

Safety runs after current danger observation and before ordinary activity selection. It can suspend any activity or act with no ordinary offers. It submits one request to ActivityCoordination; it never applies the motor itself or chooses a weapon.

```
SharedSafety/
├─ CLAUDE.md                   ownership, release conditions and limits
├─ ChooseSafetyResponse.cs     response identity, interruption and common movement requests
└─ ReachEnvironmentalSafety.cs native breathing/lava escape and stable air/landing guidance
```

Environmental response starts for lava exposure, depleted breathing reserve or an optimistic escape estimate that no longer fits remaining breath. Healthy ordinary water travel remains possible. The estimate is geometric guidance rather than a proven escape duration. Once escape starts, it retains control through emergence to a clear landing; reaching air at a jump apex does not complete it. Collision avoidance retains its response through an airborne aftermath and releases on a grounded observation without imminent collision. Downing and recovery explicitly cancel safety ownership.

Movement owns every candidate control, actual-entry check, unfinished frontier and retained prefix. Environmental escape uses the same current collision forecast as ordinary movement, so it cannot treat a known incoming projectile as absent merely because it is seeking air. If no safe prefix is established, the response reports that failure and retains its purpose; it does not claim a successful escape or downgrade the value of interrupted work.

The air target stays fixed while the body-state search progresses. Native head collision accounts for partial liquid height; body-sized passage distances guide the search around overhangs, while the landing objective remains separate from the first breathing position. Relevant terrain changes invalidate retained geometry. Capability limits come from the motor and shared movement rather than granting unearned swimming, flight or extra jumps.

Landing guidance uses passage distance too: the nearest dry floor by direct distance can be across a wall. After the first breathing position, brief re-submersion preserves the landing phase. Reverting to the nearest air pocket on every dip can refill breath indefinitely while discarding every unfinished shore search; the mirrored awning trace exposed that loop. A changed target's geometry is still revalidated within the retained phase.

This owner does not establish calibrated tolerable-damage trade-offs, complete multi-hazard consequence prediction or a universal breath-feasible route. Kiting's existing ordinary adapter remains pending transfer to shared safety. The captured-pool and awning fixtures establish their native geometry; wider combined-threat outcomes require their own evidence.
