# Nearby assistance — lighting, collection and keeping company

Keeping company is one ordinary activity in NearbyAssistance. It offers positive value while the player is alive and competes with other useful work; it is not merely a fallback for an empty board.

```
NearbyAssistance/
├─ CLAUDE.md           activity purpose, method choice and evidence limits
├─ KeepCompany.cs      reunion, resting, nearby movement and sealed-pocket roaming
├─ CollectNearbyItems.cs known drops and uncertain pot-content opportunities
├─ LightUsefulArea.cs   supplied permanent lighting offered through native placement rules
└─ PerformNearbyWorldWork.cs shared pot/torch discovery, approach and interaction methods
```

Preparation compares the existing reunion urgency with the value of staying locally active. Execution requests WithPlayer when reunion wins, or rests and selects small nearby movements otherwise. A sealed pocket uses the existing bounded roaming/retry policy. These are methods of the same purpose and preserve its activity identity. Entry and reunion discard old local destinations, so resuming after an interruption cannot revive a stroll in the previous neighbourhood.

Every request passes through position selection and shared movement. Only an explicit reunion request can qualify for distant recovery; a local stroll near player coordinates cannot. Shared safety can interrupt any method, and compatible shooting and held light remain independent hands work.

Reunion asks `../../PositionSelection/ChooseMeetingPlace.cs` for a place on the player's apparent journey priced by the companion's own routes and requests that exact tile; a paused or working player is met where they stand. Leaving the activity, or switching to the local method, releases the meeting place so its reason and flood never describe another activity's movement. Local movement keeps its random distribution and geometric neighbourhood limits; it does not check a stroll's standability or hazards, establish final contextual separation policy or explore safely in every terrain. There is no separate exploration activity.

A companion in a proven sealed pocket uses local movement without forgetting reunion. The coordinator exposes that state on the action context and cycles local roaming with reunion retries using BehaviourWeights. Keeping company's local method requests Roam and its reunion method requests WithPlayer. An incomplete path is not proof of a sealed pocket, and a model-closed region is not proof of physical impossibility in Terraria.

Collection prepares a fitting known drop and a bounded pot-content opportunity, compares them with the same shared factors as the parent, and publishes the chosen raw value and forecast. Publishing an already-discounted value would charge the trip twice. Neither comparison repeats discovery. Known drops bind the item, type and captured position, revalidating world-slot membership, availability and cargo acceptance before movement, and execution holds for a tick when the drop has moved more than a tile since it was proven, so the next preparation proves it where it now lies instead of walking to where it was. Each fitting drop proves its own excursion, nearest first: a standable contact pose, the one nearest the companion where the body grown by the contact pickup's reach touches the item; walker reach from the companion's feet to that pose; and a marginal return, refused only when the companion can reach the player now and could not from the drop, so a companion already cut off is not called stranded. A standable tile beside a drop proves none of that, and the reach that mined an ore says nothing about the pit its drop fell into. How many drops one preparation asks about and how long a verdict is reused are BehaviourWeights tunables; an undecided search publishes an unresolved offer at zero value. The offer is valued for the quantity the cargo can take (the loot sense's value scaled by the share of the stack's value that fits) and its forecast is the walk to the contact pose in pixels over walking speed, the unit mining and chopping use. A detached object can remain active after its slot is replaced; it is no longer a world drop. A newly observed replacement receives its own object identity. An arbitrary same-object mutation without a lifecycle signal is not detected as a new generation.

Pot contents remain unknown until the native operation produces them. Their prior value and handling allowance live in BehaviourWeights; approach contributes a geometric estimate rather than a certified pickup/return duration. Unknown contents require empty cargo space conservatively, whereas a known drop may fit an existing player or bag stack. Native home protection covers the pot's footprint and is rechecked at the operation, along with pot policy and capacity. The current method uses native KillTile at interaction range; weapon-driven breaking and prediction of the resulting drop locations remain separate implementation obligations.

Pot discovery and approach reuse PerformNearbyWorldWork. Dropping a method candidate does not release the collection activity's admission; the activity owner handles that lifecycle. Actual known-drop pickup remains the NPC's independent contact operation and can occur while another behaviour is active. A completed worksite can widen the allowance for its known drops, without granting nearby unknown pots the same allowance. Breaking a pot does not certify any pickup or obligate a subsequent trip.

Lighting uses the same bounded candidate/approach executor as collection's pot method. It offers only where darkness is measured: `../../WorldObservation/MeasureLightCoverage.cs` samples the area around the companion and reads light only where the active lighting engine computed it, so the area must be mostly measured and its mean below the dark level, with the companion's shown torch and a torch the player holds left out because they light an area only while carried there. A site whose own measured neighbourhood is already lit is refused, which is also what stops a second torch next to the first earning the same benefit. Underground or at night is no longer the test: it spent torches in lit caves and read a place the engine never computed as dark. Lighting reads supplied torch availability and native Smart Cursor recommendations through `../../WorldInteractions/Torch/`, ranks elevated left/right sites and may request a ground jump proved to reach the interaction and land safely. Native placement and resource consumption retain their own owner. An interruption invalidates an interaction jump; resumption cannot reuse proof from a previous take-off pose. Pot and torch operations recheck home protection at mutation rather than treating discovery permission as permanent.

The shared interaction executor classifies a found candidate as usable and asks its subclass why a refused method is refused: lighting separates the torch setting (a prohibition), darkness the engine has not measured and an area already lit (both no opportunity) and an empty supply in a measured dark area (known-unusable); pot collection separates the pot setting from a full bag. Collection publishes the classification of whichever method's raw value it publishes, and names a zero-valued known drop as usable rather than absent. One interaction is the whole job, so an attempt with a credited productive effect is complete and the companion's own — a placed torch without measured coverage, a broken pot with unobserved contents — while an abandoned approach, a lost jump take-off, an undelivered jump or a refused native call is a failed method and a target that stopped qualifying is invalid. Those give-ups are cleared when an attempt opens. A drop's attempt concludes from what the cargo actually received from that item object after the walk began, read from the inventory's transfer record, because a drop leaving the world is also what the player's pickup and despawning do: all of it received is the companion's own completion, part received with the rest gone is shared, gone with nothing received is invalid, and part received with the rest still lying there is partial. Keeping company classifies as usable while the player lives and concludes every attempt as executed, never complete.

A lighting or pot trip is offered only where the companion can come back. The shared search asks sites nearest first by the method's own cost and stops at the first whose access is proven; a site reached from a pose other than the current one must also pass `MovementQueries.RoundTrip` from the companion's feet to that pose, outward, return and breath all Yes. The walker search behind the access query runs under whichever one-way rule the previous tick's request left set, so on its own it certified a dark pit reached only by a drop after a reunion tick, the Exact request then refused to plan it, and the trip stalled until its progress window deferred it; the round trip asks both legs under its own rule, so the verdict no longer depends on the last request. A proven missing return or breath shortfall defers the site until the terrain changes or a BehaviourWeights wait passes, and names itself (`interaction-site-has-no-return`, `interaction-trip-exceeds-breath`, known-unusable) when it leaves no site; an undecided return only skips the site for that search (`interaction-trip-undecided`). How many trips one search may ask is a BehaviourWeights tunable, because each is two fresh route searches. The trip is proven at discovery and not again during the walk; a terrain change that removes the way back after discovery is caught only by the access and candidate checks execution already makes.

Candidate search and deferred-approach cleanup run during Prepare. Comparison reads captured values and targets without invoking native torch rules or repeating a jump proof. Execution retains validation and progress accounting; a comparison cannot count as another interaction attempt. The shared executor is not a fourth selectable activity or a separate movement writer.

Method discovery clears its own candidate without releasing the activity's continuation allowance. Collection can still have a valid known drop when pot discovery is disabled. The shared activity owner releases admission on replacement; failure of one method cannot erase another method's admitted purpose. Execution checks enabled policy and candidate validity before approach or mutation.

An ineligible-to-eligible transition refreshes discovery immediately. Preparation and execution share this eligibility observation: permission revoked after preparation must invalidate the same discovery state as permission revoked before preparation. Otherwise a supply or policy change can clear a target while its old deadline prevents fresh work. Unchanged eligibility retains bounded discovery cadence; per-target physical deferrals remain separate evidence.
