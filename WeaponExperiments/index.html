Twenty weapons for a companion who isn't you

Four classes across five stages of the game, each with two upgrade levels on top of its base form. The copper shortsword and the bow you start with aren't here — these are what the mastery tree unlocks.

Every weapon owns a move no other weapon owns, declares whether it is for one enemy or a crowd, and asks the companion for exactly one decision: pick a target, fire. Everything interesting happens after that, in the projectile.

Twinstrand Staff — two shards spiral around each other, cross three times, then collide.

Base kit is the copper shortsword and the bow. Everything above is a mastery-tree unlock gated on resources and companion level, scaling off your own damage rather than carrying its own, so no card states a number.

Build cost on each card is honest: trivial means a debuff or a counter on an existing projectile, moderate means one custom projectile with its own flight, and real work means it reaches into other entities or other projectiles in bulk.
