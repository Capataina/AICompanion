Twenty weapons for a companion who isn't you

Four classes across five stages of the game. The copper shortsword and the bow you start with aren't here — these are the twenty the mastery tree unlocks, and each one gets its own upgrade branch later rather than a tier-up replacement.

Every card runs its own behaviour. If you can't tell what a weapon does from the loop alone, the weapon isn't designed yet.
